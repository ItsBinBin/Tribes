# Parzival Privacy Policy

## Purpose and account owner

Parzival is a personal AI assistant operated by
Xabin Zabaljauregui using Hermes Agent.
It is for the owner's personal use, not public signup.

Contact: xabinzabalj@gmail.com

## Google data accessed and used

With the owner's authorization, Parzival accesses
Gmail messages and attachments, Calendar events,
and Google Drive files as needed for requested tasks.

These tasks include searching, summarizing, organizing,
and preparing responses. Sending messages or changing
events or files requires the owner's approval.

## Storage and processing

OAuth credentials are stored on the server running
Hermes Agent, not in this public website repository.

Requested content and generated results may be stored
in assistant conversation history, logs, downloaded
files, or notes on that server.

Relevant content may be sent to the configured AI
provider to process requests. Requests and responses
may also pass through the chat platform used by the
owner, such as Slack. Those services have their own
privacy and retention policies.

## Sharing and restrictions

Google data is used for the owner's assistant tasks,
not sold or used for advertising. It is not
intentionally published on this website.

## Retention and removal

There is no automatic deletion schedule configured
specifically for this integration. Stored history,
files, and notes remain until removed by the operator,
subject to hosting, backup, and service retention.

The owner can request removal of locally stored
Google data and credentials. Revoking access does not
automatically delete previously stored content.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke Parzival's authorization through
Google Account's third-party connections settings.

## Changes

This policy will be updated before material changes
to the integration's data-handling practices.
